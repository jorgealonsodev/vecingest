## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a rigorous, institutional utility environment designed specifically for Spanish property administration (*administradores de fincas colegiados*) and co-ownership communities. Evoking the structural discipline, trust, and predictability of high-tier commercial digital banking, the interface completely discards ornamental visual tropes. There are zero gradients, zero skeuomorphic blurs, and zero decorative drop shadows.

The tone is understated, authoritative, and direct. The interface acts as a quiet, hyper-functional ledger. Voice conventions demand clear Castilian Spanish in sentence casing without emotional punctuation, pleasantries ("por favor"), or emojis. Interactive commands always lead with a functional imperative verb (*Añadir*, *Revisar*, *Generar*, *Publicar*). Visual weight is reserved strictly for operational state transitions, legal compliance indicators (LPH mandates), financial aggregates, and transactional alerts.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture rests on high structural contrast anchored by a warm architectural white page base (`#F4F5F2`) and deep corporate navy typography (`#0F2A4A`). 

- **Primary (`#185FA5`):** Reserved strictly for primary affirmative actions, active navigational states, focus rings, and definitive hyperlinks. Only one primary button is permissible per viewport.
- **Secondary (`#5F6B7A`):** Applied to metadata, column headers, structural boundary lines, and secondary navigational items.
- **Tertiary (`#26215C`):** Used exclusively for statutory, legislative (Ley de Propiedad Horizontal), and legal-binding metadata tags.
- **Semantic Statuses:** Every notification, chip, and badge strictly pairs a tinted pastel container with a high-contrast deep tone text from the exact same family (e.g., `#0F6E56` on `#E1F5EE` for success). 
- **Voting Bars:** Segmented quotas are quantified through categorical semantic indicators (`#1D9E75` favor, `#D85A30` contra, `#B4B2A9` abstención, `#A32D2D` privados de voto).

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts the engineered, geometric idiosyncrasy of **Bricolage Grotesque** (display, section titles, and high-impact numerical metric tiles) with the systematic, neutral legibility of **IBM Plex Sans** (running copy, complex tables, and tabular records).

- **Data Alignment & Numerics:** All currency figures, participation quotas (porcentajes de cuota), legal deadlines, and dates must render using tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Scale Usage:** Public landing pages deploy `display` and `body-lg` for conversion narrative. Operational console views (`app.vecingest.app`) limit their vertical ceiling to `headline-md` (28px) for views, `headline-sm` (22px) for card headers, and rely predominantly on `body-md`, `body-sm`, and `label-md` for high information density.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout language uses a strict 8px base grid system, structured around purposeful architectural planes rather than dynamic floating layers:

- **Public Marketing Surface (`vecingest.app`):** Fixed central content constraint of 1120px with 24px horizontal gutters. Structural sections are delineated by 1px solid dividers (`#D6DAD5`) paired with 72px vertical spacing blocks.
- **Operational Console (`app.vecingest.app`):** Persistent, non-collapsible 240px left-hand navigation sidebar anchored to the viewport height. The main canvas receives 24px internal padding. High-density data tables conform to fixed 48px row heights.
- **Contextual Flyout Drawer:** Detail records and edit contexts anchor to the right edge via a dedicated 360px side pane, visually separated by a 1px vertical boundary (`#D6DAD5`).
- **Breakpoints:** 
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): Sidebar collapses into an off-canvas drawer; multi-column grid collapses to single-column; detail panels occupy full width (100vw).
  - Tablet (`768px – 1024px`): Two-column grids; detail panel docks over content.
  - Desktop (`> 1024px`): Full sidebar (240px) + fluid workspace + contextual drawer (360px).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately employs a **zero-shadow, flat-plane elevation model**. Depth and visual hierarchy are communicated strictly through:

1. **Boundary Lines:** All surfaces, cards, tables, and panels are articulated by crisp `1px solid #D6DAD5` borders.
2. **Tonal Planar Contrast:** The structural canvas resides on warm stone neutral `#F4F5F2`. Interactive and informational cards elevate by shifting to pure `#FFFFFF`. Hover states, active table selections, and focus containers step through the `#F6F4EE`, `#F1EFE8`, and `#E6F1FB` tonal container series.
3. **No Decorative Blur:** Glassmorphism, backdrop filters, and radial glow effects are prohibited across all views. Modals and overlays use an opaque solid backdrop mask (`rgba(15, 42, 74, 0.4)`) with no underlying blur.

## Shapes

Corner radii adhere to a calibrated utilitarian hierarchy:

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Form Controls:** Fixed radius of 8px (`0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Modals, and Surface Panels:** Fixed radius of 12px (`0.75rem`), enclosed by a 1px border.
- **Badges, Legal Markers, and Status Chips:** Full pill shape (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish classification metadata from actionable controls.
- **Dividers & Metric Separators:** Crisp 0px terminal bounds with fixed 1px stroke weight.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Height 44px, radius 8px, background `#185FA5`, text `#FFFFFF` (16px, weight 500). Hover `#0C447C`. Active `#0F2A4A`. Limited to exactly one instance per view to enforce clear visual priority.
- **Secondary:** Height 44px, radius 8px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D6DAD5`, text `#0F2A4A`. Hover `#F1EFE8`.
- **Destructive:** Height 44px, radius 8px, background `#FCEBEB`, border `1px solid #A32D2D`, text `#A32D2D`. Hover background `#A32D2D`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height 24px, pill-shaped (`9999px`), padding `0 10px`, typography `11px/14px 500`. Color assignments adhere to exact semantic matrices (e.g., *Abierta*: `#F1EFE8`/`#2C2C2A`; *Asignada*: `#E6F1FB`/`#0C447C`; *Resuelta*: `#E1F5EE`/`#04342C`).
- **Legal Compliance Chip:** Pill-shaped, background `#EEEDFE`, text `#26215C` (11px, weight 500). Used for LPH references (e.g., `Art. 17.1 · 1/3 del total`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Height 44px, radius 8px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D6DAD5`, text `#0F2A4A` (16px). Focus state replaces border with a clean 2px outline in `#185FA5`.
- **Labels:** Positioned strictly above fields, in `13px/18px 500` secondary text (`#5F6B7A`).
- **In-line Legal Validation:** Displayed below fields with a balance-scale icon and statutory citation in `#A32D2D` (if blocking) or `#854F0B` (if cautionary).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size 20px with 4px radius (checkbox) or circular (radio), border `1.5px solid #5F6B7A`, checked state solid `#185FA5` with white checkmark.

### Tables
- **Header:** Height 40px, text `13px/18px 500 #5F6B7A`, border-bottom `1px solid #D6DAD5`.
- **Data Rows:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border-bottom `1px solid #D6DAD5`, text `14px/20px #0F2A4A`. Selected state uses `#E6F1FB`. Numeric and currency values strictly right-aligned with tabular figures.

### Metric Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D6DAD5`, radius 12px, padding 16px. Numerical indicator set in 40px Bricolage Grotesque, label below in 14px IBM Plex Sans `#5F6B7A`. Critical deviation displays the number directly in `#A32D2D`.

### Voting Result Bars
- Dual-bar split (top: participation quotas; bottom: total headcounts). Horizontal segmented bars (height 12px, radius 4px) rendered with colors `#1D9E75` (a favor), `#D85A30` (en contra), and `#B4B2A9` (abstención), crossed by a vertical threshold pin indicating required legal quorum.
