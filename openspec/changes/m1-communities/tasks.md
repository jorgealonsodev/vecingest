`size:exception`, approved by the maintainer in-session on 2026-09-15 and
recorded in the runtime ledger (`sdd-attempt reset`, request id
`m1-pr1-size-exception-reset-001`).

than that number suggests:

| Kind | Lines |
|---|---|
| sqlc-generated (regenerated by `make gen`, not reviewed by hand) | 985 |
| Tests | 970 |
| Hand-written Go | 930 |
| Migrations / SQL | 282 |

The exception was taken rather than splitting further because a two-way split
(schema, then `authz`) leaves **both** halves above 400, so it would not have
satisfied the policy either — and the only split that reaches 400 would
separate `AssertScopedRegistration`'s A1/A2/A3 from the `scoped.*`
constructors they exist to enforce, merging a half-built guard into the chain.
That is the specific risk `feature-branch-chain` was chosen to avoid.

The exception covers **PR1 only**. WU-2 through WU-8 are each forecast inside
the 400-line budget and are expected to hold to it; WU-6 (invitations, 20
tasks) is the most likely to need re-checking before it is opened.
Chain strategy: feature-branch-chain
400-line budget risk: High

Tracker branch `feature/m1-communities` accumulates the final integration; PR1 targets the
tracker branch, each later PR targets the immediate previous PR's branch, and only the tracker
merges to `main`. This is chosen over stacked-to-main because M1 introduces the tenant-isolation
enforcement mechanism itself — a defect discovered in PR3 must not already be live on `main`
through PR1/PR2.

## Phase 10: PRD Corrections, Security Gate, Checkpoint A Closure (WU-8, PR8 — document discipline)

- [ ] 10.1 Corregir `PRD_go.md` §7.7 (español): sustituir "un handler sin ella no compila para rutas con ámbito (interfaz obligatoria)" por la redacción del diseño D-1/D-2, NO por la de la propuesta, que quedó superada.
